Action-credit sensitivity

2017–18 shot-ending sequence pilot · attacking-only diagnostic · not a player ranking or approved credit rule

The direct-actor and equal-participant methods allocate the same modeled sequence total, but player ordering differs. Whiskers show 95% match-cluster bootstrap intervals from 1,000 fixture resamples; each cutoff keeps its full-sample cohort fixed.

Minimum sequencesPlayers includedSpearman ρ · observed; bootstrap median [95% interval]Rank movement · observed; bootstrap median [95% interval]

Observed across all 1,970 IDs, Spearman ρ is 0.812 and median rank movement is 171 places; at 25+ sequences, these are 0.881 and 66.5 places among 286 IDs. Match-bootstrap medians at those cutoffs are 0.786 [0.763, 0.810] and 144 [124, 170], then 0.862 [0.829, 0.890] and 76 [61, 94.5]. Neither method is shown to be more accurate. Intervals resample matches but condition on the fitted model and do not capture player-value or model-training uncertainty. Sequence boundaries are proxies, the player IDs are not reconciled to the eight-season site profiles, and the sample omits most defending and goalkeeper actions.

What an “action change” contains

Consecutive-event replay · expected-points accounting diagnostic · not isolated player credit

Clock, own events & opponent events all move state

Each successive event updates both teams’ expected-points state. The observed step includes elapsed time since the previous event. The clock-only line is a counterfactual that freezes the previous event features while advancing time; the remaining event-update residual depends on that assumption.

Adjusted-lineup feasibility

OpenPlay · 2018–19 to 2024–25 held-out seasons · match points

No stable lineup gain

Across 14,328 matches with valid starting XIs, adding player-presence features did not reliably improve expected-points forecasts beyond pre-match team context. At a fixed exploratory shrinkage setting, context performed better in all seven future-season folds. Selecting shrinkage using earlier seasons only made the average difference nearly zero and inconsistent across folds.

Fixed shrinkageStarting XI: +0.0185 log loss · context better in 7/7 seasons
Earlier-season tuningStarting XI: −0.00033 average difference · mixed
Player coverage11.4%–19.2% of held-out starter slots were unseen IDs
InterpretationAssociation only · not action residual or player impact

Positive differences mean the lineup model had higher log loss. Realized-minute features are post-match and can reflect substitutions or match events. This result does not rule out better adjusted models; it does not estimate player value or teammate uplift. The required action-only versus action-plus-lineup comparison remains open.
