# WAR data-gate closeout — 2026-11-05

## Decision

The four requested gates are **not all passed**, so publishing player WAR would still overstate the evidence. This closeout reruns the local identity, position-slot, goalkeeper-linkage, and replacement-pilot audits, then checks newly surfaced public-data leads. It does not choose a formula, promote any identity candidate, infer tactical roles, or write player values. Work remains in the existing repository; no new repository is needed.

## Reruns performed

1. `scripts/audit_event_player_identity_candidates.py` completed for all five leagues and rewrote the aggregate-only `reports/action_event_identity_coverage.csv`. Strict exact-name/full-date candidates cover 1,603 of 2,618 active Wyscout player IDs across the five 2017–18 competitions; same-birth-date/two-token candidate IDs cover 2,310. This is candidate coverage, not identity precision. The separate event-season audit remains stronger for exact-key player-season matching; neither result licenses fuzzy auto-linking.
2. `scripts/audit_openplay_formation_slots.py` completed on all 40 source cells with expanding time splits. It screened 135,598 formation records, excluded 418 invalid intervals and seven array mismatches, and retained the previously documented 93.49% within-five-minute row reconciliation / 90.39% supported-minute coverage. Aggregate-only local outputs contain no player IDs.
3. `scripts/audit_openplay_keeper_shot_linkage.py` completed on 35 later-season cells: 300,005 of 314,337 shots received a unique interval-based keeper; 102,129 of 107,066 on-target shots (95.39%) were assigned. The model remains uncalibrated and assignments lack an independent sample review.
4. `scripts/audit_replacement_pilot.py --expanded` completed: seven purposive examples, zero classified replacement-level, no baseline estimated, and no player statistics changed.
5. `scripts/run_openplay_keeper_shot_outcome_pilot.py` completed seven chronological holdouts (2018–19 through 2024–25), comparing pre-shot features with pre-shot plus target-location features. Pooled Brier changed from 0.172004 to 0.172004 (slightly worse by 0.00000005), log loss from 0.519666 to 0.519276, and AUC from 0.754940 to 0.755230. The Brier difference changed direction across seasons and league-season cells; no keeper IDs or values were used. Results and limitations are in [the keeper shot-outcome pilot](openplay_keeper_shot_outcome_pilot_2026-11-05.md).
6. `scripts/audit_openplay_keeper_direct_lineup_linkage.py` completed: direct GK lineup intervals cover 310,575/314,337 shots and 105,904/107,066 on-target shots (98.91%), with no ambiguous assignments. All 25,016 GK-position rows have interval length matching reported minutes; this is internal same-source consistency, not validation. It flags 244 GK substitutions whose incoming keeper has generic `Sub` position, leaving 1,614 later shots in those team-matches unassigned. See [the direct lineup audit](openplay_keeper_direct_lineup_audit_2026-11-05.md).
7. Follow-up script `scripts/audit_openplay_keeper_substitute_linkage.py` applies a strict exact-minute cross-table rule to those substitutions. It recovers 195 unique positive intervals of 244, assigning 415 additional on-target shots without conflicts and raising coverage to 106,319/107,066 (99.30%). The original six-case purposive check confirmed the keeper pairs but found clock differences. A later league-stratified 40-case sample (eight per league) also confirmed the pair in all 40 candidates; 7/32 comparable numeric times aligned exactly, 25 differed by 1–4 minutes, six reports described a half-time change, and two did not give a precise minute. This pair-check does not validate all shot-to-keeper intervals or estimate keeper value; the formation, lineup, and shot data remain from the same publisher. See the [substitute linkage audit](openplay_keeper_substitute_linkage_2026-11-06.md).

## Public source checks

- **Mendeley substitution dataset:** the publisher page declares CC BY 4.0 and describes five-league coverage in 2019/20–2022/23, 7,203 matches, substitutions, incoming/outgoing positions, and 1,311,302 team-minute observations. The downloaded package was schema-audited: its 115-column RDS has 7,203 matches and 20 league-season cells but only team-minute aggregate substitution indicators and a `NoBenchPlayer` flag; it contains no player IDs/names or player-role intervals. Therefore it cannot cross-check player-level roles or pass the actual-position-minutes gate, and it was not joined to FutWAR. Reproduction: `Rscript scripts/audit_mendeley_substitution_source.R <path-to-final_data.rds>`. The source file remains ignored under `data/raw/`. See [file-level follow-up](position_minutes_source_followup_2026-11-04.md) and the [publisher page](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/m3v93kjp7p/1).
- **Footy Cap:** its feed says it is free to reuse with a visible link, and each figure includes source, confidence and as-of date. But it is club-level, linked historical transfer coverage is sparse, and underlying figures retain their own sources' rights. It does not establish a season-start affordable player pool. Source: [data feed and terms](https://footycap.com/data-feed/).
- **Cornerflick:** it offers event and lineup files and declares CC BY 4.0 for its compilation, while expressly saying underlying provider terms still apply and large-scale redistribution needs further checking. That does not currently clear it as a replacement source for public player-level scoring. Source: [data page and terms](https://cornerflick.com/data).
- **New 2025/26 sample:** the surfaced Champions League SkillCorner/Hugging Face data is marked CC BY-NC 4.0, requires acceptance of access conditions, and covers a different competition; it is not suitable as the missing public Big Five domestic final test. Source: [dataset card and license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/peggy44/ChampionsLeague25-26).

## Next actions that can genuinely close gates

1. **Identity:** create a reproducible stratified random review sample from the 16,725 exact-key OpenPlay links; check each against an independent match roster/official source, report precision with a confidence interval, and review unmatched/discrepant strata separately. Keep unreviewed IDs unlinked.
2. **Position minutes:** the Mendeley package is now acquired and excluded for player-level role work because its analysis file has no player identity/role intervals. For full scope, obtain independently timestamped player-role intervals or use only a role definition supported for every minute. Do not fill intervals from FIFA/EA roster positions; roster position is not the role played in a match.
3. **Goalkeepers:** independently validate the direct-lineup shot links, prioritizing minute boundaries and the 244 team-matches with a subbed-off GK and generic incoming `Sub` labels. Do not infer replacement keeper identity. Then fit and validate a keeper-specific outcome model; keep it separate until a shared scale is empirically supported.
4. **Replacement access:** continue only if a source gives season-timed availability plus cost components for actual candidates. Otherwise keep the team-accessible view explicitly unavailable and proceed only with the two average benchmarks, labelled above-average comparisons.
5. **Final test:** freeze candidate definitions and reserve the next compatible in-scope Big Five season that has reusable match/event data. Do not treat another league's competition sample, a random event split, or the reused 2024–25 fold as the untouched final test.
